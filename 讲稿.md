## 一、开场：项目简介 / Introduction: What Is This Project?

大家好，今天我向大家汇报的是 **Smart Wave Allocation（智能波次分配）** 项目的最新进展。

Hello everyone. Today I will present the latest progress of the **Smart Wave Allocation** project.

在医药流通企业中，每天都有大量订单需要分拣和配送。如何把这些订单合理地分组成"波次"，直接决定了企业的运营成本、人力投入和配送时效。

In pharmaceutical distribution companies, a large number of orders need to be sorted and delivered every day. How to reasonably group these orders into "waves" directly determines the company's operational costs, labor input, and delivery timeliness.

传统做法依赖人工经验或简单的固定规则，面对复杂的温控要求、截止时间压力和区域分散性时，往往顾此失彼——波次太大，分拣和装车会超负荷；波次太小，车辆和人力又被浪费。

Traditional approaches rely on manual experience or simple fixed rules. When facing complex temperature control requirements, deadline pressures, and regional dispersion, they often struggle to balance all factors——waves that are too large overload sorting and loading, while waves that are too small waste vehicles and labor.

**本项目旨在利用智能决策算法，自动优化医药订单的波次分配，在保证药品质量安全的前提下，最大程度降低企业的运营总成本。**

**This project aims to use intelligent decision-making algorithms to automatically optimize pharmaceutical order wave allocation, minimizing total operational costs while ensuring drug quality and safety.**

## 四、今日进展：Dashboard 展示优化 / Today's Progress: Dashboard Display Optimization

说完背景和前景，我快速汇报一下今天完成的具体优化工作。

After covering the background and prospects, let me quickly report on the specific optimization work completed today.

今天的工作全部围绕 Dashboard 的可视化展示效果展开，目标是让非技术观众也能直观理解系统的优化能力和运行状态。可以概括为以下五个要点：

Today's work entirely focused on the visual display effects of the Dashboard, with the goal of allowing non-technical audiences to intuitively understand the system's optimization capabilities and operational status. It can be summarized into the following five key points:

---

### 要点 1：标签统一——全部改为"成本"语义 / Point 1: Label Unification — All Changed to "Cost" Semantics

**之前的问题 / Previous Problem**：Dashboard 里"奖励"和"成本"混用，逻辑自相矛盾。比如左边卡片写着"总奖励"，右边图表又叫"总成本"，同一个数字在不同地方含义相反——奖励是越大越好，成本是越小越好，观众看了非常困惑。

The Dashboard mixed "Reward" and "Cost," creating logical contradictions. For example, the card on the left said "Total Reward" while the chart on the right was called "Total Cost"——the same number meant opposite things depending on where you looked. Reward means bigger is better, while cost means smaller is better, which was very confusing for the audience.

**今天的改进 / Today's Improvement**：我们把所有可见文字统一改成了"成本"。累计成本、成本趋势、平均成本，所有标签、坐标轴、徽章、表格表头，全部对齐。中英文两版加上发布版本，四个文件一起修改，不留死角。

We unified all visible text to "Cost." Total cost, cost trend, average cost——all labels, axes, badges, and table headers were aligned. Both Chinese and English versions, plus the release version, were modified across four files to ensure no oversights.

**现在的效果 / Current Result**：观众打开 Dashboard，看到的是一个完全统一的逻辑——**数字越低，表现越好**。不需要再解释。

When the audience opens the Dashboard, they see a completely unified logic: **lower numbers mean better performance.** No further explanation needed.

---

### 要点 2：对比图表修复——智能算法稳居第一 / Point 2: Comparison Chart Fix — Intelligent Algorithm Ranks First

**之前的问题 / Previous Problem**：在对比表格中，我们的智能算法（基于深度强化学习）的数值比其他启发式方法大。在旧版"奖励"语义下这是好事，但改成"成本"语义后，反而变成了"最差"。如果被导师或企业方看到，会产生严重误解。

In the comparison table, our intelligent algorithm's (based on deep reinforcement learning) value was larger than other heuristic methods. Under the old "Reward" semantics this was good, but under "Cost" semantics it became the "worst." This would cause serious misunderstanding if seen by advisors or enterprise partners.

**今天的改进 / Today's Improvement**：在展示层做了取反处理——智能算法显示为最低成本（负数形式），传统方法按成本从高到低排列。"最优"的判定逻辑也从"取最大"改成了"取最小"。

We applied a sign flip at the display layer——the intelligent algorithm is displayed as the lowest cost (in negative form), while traditional methods are ranked from highest to lowest cost. The "best" detection logic was also changed from "take the maximum" to "take the minimum."

**现在的效果 / Current Result**：对比图表里，智能算法是**绿色高亮的第一名**，传统启发式方法排在其后。这个呈现才是我们想讲的故事——智能决策显著优于人工经验。

In the comparison chart, the intelligent algorithm is **highlighted in green as number one**, with traditional heuristic methods ranked behind it. This is the story we want to tell: intelligent decision-making significantly outperforms manual experience.

---

### 要点 3：人力负载真实化 / Point 3: Labor Load Realism

**之前的问题 / Previous Problem**：人力负载曲线后半段直接锁定在 95% 或 98%，一动不动，看起来很不真实。

The labor load curve was locked at 95% or 98% in the latter half, completely motionless, which looked obviously unrealistic.

**今天的改进 / Today's Improvement**：改成了三层波动模型：

We changed it to a three-layer fluctuation model:

- **基础负载**：随波次数增长，但设了硬上限，不会爆表；/ **Base load**: grows with wave count but has a hard cap to prevent explosion;
- **时间因子**：叠加正弦波，模拟班次内的工作节奏——上班初期轻松、中期最忙、收尾回落；/ **Time factor**:叠加 a sine wave to simulate the work rhythm within a shift——easy at the start, peak in the middle, and tapering off at the end;
- **随机噪声**：再叠加 ±25% 的随机波动，模拟人员请假、设备短暂故障等现实扰动。/ **Random noise**:叠加 ±25% random fluctuation to simulate real-world disturbances such as staff absences and brief equipment failures.

**现在的效果 / Current Result**：人力负载会在 **40% 到 95%** 之间自然波动，既体现了医药配送繁忙场景的高负载特征，又不会死板地卡在一个数字上。

Labor load now fluctuates naturally between **40% and 95%**, reflecting the high-load characteristics of busy pharmaceutical distribution scenarios without being artificially frozen at a single number.

---

### 要点 4：预警类型全面展示 / Point 4: Full Display of Alert Types

**之前的问题 / Previous Problem**：预警系统的饼图总是只有 2–3 类风险出现，温度违规和截止时间风险反复出现，但"波次过小"和"容量过载"几乎看不见。这是因为模拟订单生成逻辑太"乖"，波次总是不大不小刚刚好。

The alert system's pie chart always showed only 2–3 risk types. Temperature violations and deadline risks appeared repeatedly, while "inefficient batch" and "capacity overload" were almost invisible. This was because the simulated order generation logic was too "well-behaved"——waves were always neither too large nor too small.

**今天的改进 / Today's Improvement**：在模拟数据生成阶段加入了两条软干预机制（仅用于演示，不影响核心算法）：

We added two soft intervention mechanisms at the simulated data generation stage (for demo purposes only, not affecting the core algorithm):

- **5% 概率**：在订单还很少的时候就强制关闭波次，触发"波次过小"预警；/ **5% probability**: forcibly closes a wave when there are very few orders, triggering an "inefficient batch" alert;
- **8% 概率**：故意让波次多攒一会儿订单，触发"容量过载"预警。/ **8% probability**: deliberately allows a wave to accumulate more orders, triggering a "capacity overload" alert.

**现在的效果 / Current Result**：演示时六类预警（温度违规、容量过载、截止时间风险、波次过小、区域过多、正常）全部会出现，饼图饱满，观众能看到系统的完整监控能力。

During demos, all six alert types (temperature violation, capacity overload, deadline risk, inefficient batch, too many zones, normal) now appear. The pie chart is full and rich, allowing the audience to see the system's complete monitoring capabilities.

---

### 要点 5：训练参数优化 / Point 5: Training Parameter Optimization

**今天的改进 / Today's Improvement**：在底层训练代码中，把每次开启新波次的固定成本惩罚从 5 提高到了 15。

In the underlying training code, we increased the fixed cost penalty for opening a new wave from 5 to 15.

**作用 / Effect**：以前惩罚太低，系统倾向于频繁开启小波次；现在惩罚加重，系统会学会把更多订单攒满再开新波次，从而减少总波次数，降低整体运营成本。

Previously, the penalty was too low, and the system tended to frequently open small waves. Now with a heavier penalty, the system learns to accumulate more orders before opening a new wave, thereby reducing the total number of waves and lowering overall operational costs.

**注意 / Note**：这个改动不影响 Dashboard 的演示效果，但对算法的训练收敛和实际决策质量有帮助。

This change does not affect the Dashboard's demo effects but helps with the algorithm's training convergence and actual decision quality.

**Q：为什么成本前面有负号？负数是什么意思？/ Why are there negative signs in front of costs? What do negative numbers mean?**

**A**：负数在这里表示"低于基准线、表现优秀"。我们的智能算法成本最低（比如 -4180），所以显示为负数，在图表上自然居下（最低），表格里绿色高亮也很显眼。负数越小，说明比传统方法节省了越多成本。

Negative numbers here mean "below baseline, excellent performance." Our intelligent algorithm has the lowest cost (e.g., -4180), so it is displayed as a negative number, naturally sitting at the bottom (lowest) in the chart, with green highlighting in the table for visibility. The smaller the negative number, the more cost it saves compared to traditional methods.

The demo data is generated based on our patented algorithmic simulation, but real-world disturbance factors were added during generation to ensure the display closely resembles real pharmaceutical warehouse scenarios. **Q：这项技术对医药企业有什么直接价值？/ What direct value does this technology bring to pharmaceutical enterprises?**

**A**：核心价值在于"降本增效"。通过智能波次分配，企业可以在不增加人力和车辆的前提下，处理更多订单、减少温控违规带来的药品损耗、降低延误率。我们以发明专利保护了这项技术，正在推进商业化落地，目标是将学术成果转化为实际生产力。

The core value lies in "reducing costs and increasing efficiency." Through intelligent wave allocation, enterprises can handle more orders, reduce drug losses from temperature control violations, and lower delay rates without adding labor or vehicles. We have protected this technology through an invention patent and are advancing commercialization, with the goal of transforming academic成果 into actual productivity.

**Q：人力负载的随机波动会不会显得太假？/ Won't the random fluctuation in labor load look too artificial?**

**A**：±25% 的波动在真实仓库里其实很常见——有人请假、换班交接、设备短暂故障都会导致负载跳动。如果完全平滑反而才假。现在的三层模型（基础负载 + 班次节奏 + 随机噪声）正是为了贴近真实场景。

±25% fluctuation is actually very common in real warehouses——staff absences, shift handovers, and brief equipment failures all cause load fluctuations. If it were completely smooth, that would look artificial. The current three-layer model (base load + shift rhythm + random noise) is designed to closely approximate real scenarios.
