Introduction
The case companies and all numbers are made up for educational purposes. Any resemblance to
real enterprises is purely coincidental.
This casebook is designed for the Digital Innovation course (SDC MSc Innovation Management),
featuring two enterprises and two AI development challenges.
Each case provides rich business data, system context, and challenge descriptions. Student teams
have 4 weeks to build runnable prototypes using the Kimi AI model.
Cases are calibrated for MSc Innovation Management level, balancing technical innovation with
business analysis. Suitable for predominantly non-technical teams (5 students, 1-2 with technical
background).

Case 1: Smart Warehouse Sorting & Dispatch System
Company Background
"A Large Pharmaceutical Logistics Enterprise" (hereinafter "the Enterprise") is one of China's leading
pharmaceutical distribution companies, with over 40 years in the industry and annual revenue
exceeding RMB 100 billion.
The Enterprise operates a nationwide "integrated wholesale-retail-storage-distribution"
pharmaceutical logistics service system, with 128 modern logistics centers, 856 terminal distribution
stations, 2.65 million square meters of GSP-standard warehouse space, annual throughput exceeding
90 million cases, covering 28 provinces and over 94% of administrative regions.
In 2025, the Enterprise achieved revenue of RMB 138.76 billion, serving 12,800 public hospitals,
5,832 chain pharmacies, 196,500 independent pharmacies, and 357,400 primary healthcare
institutions.

Challenge Description
The Enterprise's warehousing system faces these core challenges:
• SKU complexity: Among 423,600 product lines, ~38% are near-expiry managed items with
varying temperature requirements (ambient, cool, cold, frozen), making sorting rules highly
complex.
• Order fragmentation: The B2B platform sees >36,000 daily active customers, mostly small-
batch multi-SKU orders averaging 3-5 items per order, yet total daily orders exceed 90,000.
• Time pressure: The >88% next-day delivery promise requires same-day sorting and dispatch;
peak periods (e.g., flu season) can spike order volume by 280%.
• Labor bottleneck: Sorting relies heavily on manual labor; peak periods require 2.5x temporary
staffing, with ~0.35% error rate and annual return losses exceeding RMB 9 million.
• Inventory turnover: Some pharmaceutical products have near-expiry scrap rates of ~4.8%,
urgently requiring smart dispatch systems to optimize FIFO strategy.
Task Requirements
Design and develop a smart warehouse sorting & dispatch system with these core features:
• Smart wave allocation: Auto-generate sorting waves based on order characteristics, product
attributes, and warehouse layout to optimize picking paths.
