Case 2: Pharmaceutical Demand Forecasting & Intelligent Replenishment
Company Background
Same enterprise as Case 1. The Enterprise operates China's largest out-of-hospital (OOH) sales network, with OOH sales accounting for >68%. Its B2B e-commerce platform achieved 2025 sales revenue of RMB 19.847 billion (+19.86% YoY).
The platform has >580,000 registered users, 340,000 active users, and 95.1% terminal customer satisfaction. It integrates intelligent search, AI customer service, smart procurement, and automated order verification.

Challenge Description
The Enterprise's pharmaceutical supply chain management faces these challenges:
•  High demand volatility: Pharmaceutical demand is affected by seasonal diseases (flu, allergy seasons), public health emergencies, and policy changes (volume procurement), resulting in low forecast accuracy.
•  Long-tail SKUs: Among 380,000 SKUs, 78% are low-frequency items; some sell fewer than 100 boxes annually, yet stockouts lead to customer churn.
•  Stockouts and overstock coexist: Hot-selling products frequently stock out (annual loss ~RMB 420m), while slow-moving items pile up with turnover exceeding 165 days for some categories.
•  Decentralized replenishment: 28,600 franchise pharmacies each make independent ordering decisions, lacking unified smart restock recommendations; most rely on experience-based ordering.
•  Policy shock: National volume procurement policies have reduced selected drug prices by ~51% on average, causing volatile sales that break traditional forecasting models.
Task Requirements
Design and develop a demand forecasting & intelligent replenishment system:
•  Multi-factor demand forecasting: Integrate seasonality, epidemiological data, policy changes, and historical sales to forecast 14-30 day demand by category.
•  Intelligent replenishment recommendations: Generate personalized restock lists for hospitals, chain pharmacies, and independent stores with suggested order quantities and optimal timing.
•  Inventory health diagnosis: Analyze terminal inventory structure, identify slow-moving, fast-moving, and near-expiry items, and provide reallocation suggestions.
•  Policy impact simulation: Model how volume procurement and other policy changes affect specific drug demand.
•  Alerts & notifications: Proactively push alerts for impending stockouts or inventory overages.
Deliverables
•  Runnable forecasting & replenishment system prototype
•  One SKU-level forecast demo (e.g., 3-month sales trend prediction vs. actual for a chronic disease medication)
•  Business feasibility analysis (target users, value proposition, go-to-market strategy)
