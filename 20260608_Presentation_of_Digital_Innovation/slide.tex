\section{Operational Bottleneck}

\begin{frame}{The Operational Bottleneck}
  \framesubtitle{Diagnostic Overview of the Pharmaceutical Supply Chain}
  
  The current warehousing paradigm is fundamentally constrained by several critical factors:
  \vspace{0.5cm}
  \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Order Fragmentation:} Managing over 90,000 daily orders with a high degree of SKU complexity (averaging 3-5 SKUs per order).
      \item \textbf{Inventory Vulnerability:} Urgent requirement for strict First-In-First-Out (FIFO) protocols to mitigate near-expiry pharmaceutical waste.
      \item \textbf{Labor Scalability:} A disproportionate reliance on temporary labor during seasonal peaks, reaching a 2.5x operational threshold.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{0.3cm}
  \textit{Conclusion:} Heuristic scheduling is mathematically insufficient to resolve these concurrent constraints.
\end{frame}

\section{Algorithmic Architecture}

\begin{frame}{Core Algorithmic Architecture}
  \framesubtitle{Birkhoff-von Neumann \& KGDRL}
  
  To solve the multi-objective wave allocation problem, we implemented an advanced mathematical pipeline:
  \vspace{0.3cm}
  \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Birkhoff-von Neumann (BvN) Decomposition:} Utilized to untangle high-density, multi-SKU permutation matrices into executable deterministic routing schedules.
      \item \textbf{Knowledge Graph-based Deep Reinforcement Learning (KGDRL):} A robust PPO agent trained to dynamically adapt picking routes based on real-time temperature-zone capacities.
      \item \textbf{Theoretical Defense:} The algorithmic foundations strictly adhere to methodologies validated within recent IEEE journal and conference literature, ensuring academic rigor and systemic stability.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
